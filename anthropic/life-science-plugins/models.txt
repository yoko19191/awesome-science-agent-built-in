# ==============================================================================
# 1. Protein Binder Re-Prediction & Structural Validation Models (binder-gauntlet)
# ==============================================================================

Model: Boltz-1 / Boltz-2
Category: All-Atom Biomolecular Complex Re-Prediction
Provider / Source: MIT CSAIL / Valence Labs
Framework: PyTorch
Description: Independent all-atom validation engine assessing target-binder interface geometry, complex pLDDT, and interface affinity without relying on training priors from the design model.
Resource: NVIDIA GPU (>= 16 GB VRAM) on local workstation or cluster.
Associated Skills:
  - binder-gauntlet

Model: AlphaFold2 / ColabFold
Category: Orthogonal Structural Verification & ipTM Assessment
Provider / Source: DeepMind / ColabFold
Framework: JAX / PyTorch
Description: Used as an orthogonal structural verification model to calculate interface predicted TM-score (ipTM), interface contacts, and binder stability metrics.
Resource: NVIDIA GPU (>= 16 GB VRAM).
Associated Skills:
  - binder-gauntlet

Model: Chai-1
Category: Multi-Modal Complex Co-Folding Cross-Validation
Provider / Source: Chai Discovery
Framework: PyTorch
Description: Fast single-sequence and complex co-folding engine for cross-validating protein-protein binding interfaces.
Resource: NVIDIA GPU (>= 24 GB VRAM).
Associated Skills:
  - binder-gauntlet

Model: ProteinMPNN / SolubleMPNN
Category: Sequence Recovery & Decoy Generation
Provider / Source: Baker Lab / IPD
Framework: PyTorch
Description: Inverse folding model used to compute sequence recovery rates across binding interfaces and generate control decoys.
Resource: GPU or CPU.
Associated Skills:
  - binder-gauntlet

Model: RFdiffusion
Category: Negative Control & Scrambled Binder Generator
Provider / Source: Baker Lab / IPD
Framework: PyTorch
Description: Generates structurally plausible non-binding decoy backbones to test false-positive rates of computational binding predictions.
Resource: GPU (>= 16 GB VRAM).
Associated Skills:
  - binder-gauntlet


# ==============================================================================
# 2. Clinical Trial Probability of Success & Biostatistical Models
# ==============================================================================

Model: Monte Carlo Hazard & Event Simulation Model (clinical-trial-forecast)
Category: Parametric Survival & Event-Driven Trial Forecasting
Provider / Source: clinical-trial-forecast internal simulation engine
Framework: Python / NumPy / SciPy
Description: Parametric hazard rate model (Exponential, Weibull, Gompertz) simulating patient enrollment, dropout, event accumulation, and primary endpoint readout under realistic clinical hazard ratios.
Resource: Multi-core CPU.
Associated Skills:
  - clinical-trial-forecast

Model: Trial Probability of Success (POS) Multi-Factor Heuristic Model
Category: Probabilistic Evidence Synthesis Engine
Provider / Source: clinical-trial-forecast
Framework: Python
Description: Integrates historical phase transition benchmarks, biological mechanism validation, biomarker patient stratification, and statistical power to calculate posterior trial success probabilities.
Resource: CPU.
Associated Skills:
  - clinical-trial-forecast

Model: Biostatistical Sample Size Calculation Engine (clinical-trial-protocol-skill)
Category: Hypothesis Testing & Power Estimation Models
Provider / Source: clinical-trial-protocol-skill (`sample_size_calculator.py`)
Framework: Python / SciPy / Statsmodels
Description: Computes required sample sizes and statistical power for:
  - Two-arm continuous superiority, non-inferiority, and equivalence trials (t-test / Z-test)
  - Two-arm binary proportion superiority and non-inferiority trials (Farrington-Manning / Chi-squared)
  - Time-to-event survival trials (Schoenfeld log-rank formula with accrual and dropout adjustments)
Resource: CPU.
Associated Skills:
  - clinical-trial-protocol-skill
