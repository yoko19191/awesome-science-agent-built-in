# Required rare-disease reporting policy

This policy applies to report facts, prose, illustrations and the final answer. Research decision support only: never emit a diagnosis, diagnostic probability or ACMG/AMP variant classification. Preserve each hypothesis's evidence state, reason codes, inheritance/phase safeguards, evidence precedence and source-version provenance. A concordant state is not clinical confirmation; unresolved evidence stays unresolved in prose and images.

Read `references/bibliography.json` before preparing the report. Cite only its five numbered references, with the exact pinned bibliographic text and DOI assigned to each number. Include inline [1], [2], [3], [4], [5] before the References section, in numeric order. The References section must contain exactly that ordered numbered set. Do not retrieve, add, remove, reorder, invent or substitute sources. Do not invoke literature search, web search, web fetch or any network retrieval during the analysis or report. Stop if the supplied material cannot support a citation. The obsolete DOI `10.1101/111039` is forbidden.

Use these exact visible headings in this order: Introduction, Methods, Results, Conclusions, Next steps, References. Explain the case question and supplied inputs in Introduction; methods, gate logic and source limitations in Methods; actual ranked hypotheses and uncertainty in Results; research-only interpretation in Conclusions; evidence-resolving actions in Next steps. Preserve methodological citations rather than presenting them as case-specific verification.

Use the immutable `hypotheses.tsv`, `evidence_dossier.json`, `reanalysis_diff.json`, and `figures/evidence_completeness.png` as the declared presentation sources. Report a supplied-prior comparison when available; otherwise state reanalysis was not requested. Do not recompute rankings or infer phase or inheritance in presentation. Link every claim to the relevant recorded evidence state and disclose missing or conflicting evidence. The infographic must not strengthen a hypothesis beyond its recorded state.

Build `report_facts.json` by copying `n_hypotheses_total`, `n_concordant`, `n_incomplete`, `n_conflicting`, and `n_abstained` from `evidence_dossier.json`'s `summary` object into identically named top-level facts fields. Do not recompute these counts. Their integer partition must sum to `n_hypotheses_total`, as declared in `skill_spec.json`. Also copy `source_outputs` exactly from the canonical receipt's analysis-output path/hash map. A facts file containing only nested summary counts does not satisfy the report accounting contract.

The unmodified canonical helper checks integrity and objective PDF properties. It does not enforce this domain bibliography or judge scientific/visual quality. The campaign's external acceptance harness checks the exact heading/citation/DOI requirements against extracted final PDF text; independent inspection checks scientific meaning, source use, all rendered pages and the run trace. Completing the canonical receipt alone is not final acceptance.
